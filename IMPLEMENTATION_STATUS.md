## Benchmark State & Boundary Declaration

- **Evaluated Datasets**:
  1. `stephenson_2021_healthy_pbmc`: 78,959 cells across 23 donors; 12 Cell Ontology classes (circulating blood).
  2. `gse164690_hnscc`: 136,881 cells across 18 patients; 14 Cell Ontology classes (solid head & neck tumor microenvironment).
- **Total Evaluated Cohort**: **215,840 single cells** evaluated across **100 GNN runs** and **12 matched MLP baseline runs**.
- **Frozen Split Contracts**:
  - `splits/stephenson_2021_healthy_pbmc/site_stratified_seed42.json` (12 train, 6 val, 5 test donors).
  - `splits/gse164690_hnscc/hpv_stratified_seed42.json` (9 train, 4 val, 5 test donors; 66,968 train, 30,215 val, 39,698 test cells).
- **Benchmark Findings**:
  - GCN systematically underperforms MLP by ~2.0% across all graphs in both datasets (0 of 45 positive comparisons).
  - GraphSAGE reaches near-exact parity with MLP (within 0.1–0.9%), but never surpasses it.
  - Symmetrical GCN collapses on randomized controls (macro-F1 ≈ 0.03–0.05), whereas GraphSAGE retains resilience (macro-F1 ≈ 0.81–0.89) via its separate $W_1 x$ root transformation.
